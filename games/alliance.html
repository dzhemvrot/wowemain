How to play
  • 15×15 grid · start with 3 hearts, 1 AP, range 3
  • Actions (spend AP): Move (1), Give 1 AP in range (1), Shoot (1), Upgrade range (2)
  • Range is a 5×5 area plus one extra tile in each orthogonal direction (3 orthogonal, 2 diagonal). Upgrading expands the same pattern.
  • Turns last 5 minutes. Living players get +1 AP at the start of each turn.
  • If more than ⅔ of living players press Ready, a 10-second countdown starts the next turn early. If no living player has AP left, a 30-second countdown starts. Host can also force the next turn.
  • Hearts, AP, range and who hit whom are secret. You can see your own range on the board; others cannot see yours. Only positions and final blows are public.
  • Public chat is open; DMs are end-to-end encrypted on your device.
  • Win: last player standing, or all living players choose World peace.

How to play (quick)
  • 15×15 grid · start with 3 hearts, 0 AP, range 3
  • Actions (spend AP): Move (1), Give 1 AP to someone in range (1), Shoot (1), Upgrade range (2)
  • Hearts, AP, range and who hit whom are secret. Only positions and final blows are public.
  • Last player standing (or negotiated standings) wins.
